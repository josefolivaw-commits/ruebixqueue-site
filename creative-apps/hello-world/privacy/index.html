Privacy policy

Effective November 11, 2026

The short version: Hello World does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you make stays on your device.

This policy explains how Hello World (the “app”), made by Rue Bix Queue LLC (“we”, “us”), handles information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not send any information about you, your device or how you use the app to us or to anyone else. It does not use analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.

Information stored on your device

Your settings, saved words, songs and mixer levels are stored only on your device so the app remembers them between sessions. We cannot see this information. Deleting the app deletes it.

Permissions the app may ask for

  • Bluetooth: only to connect to Bluetooth MIDI devices you choose, such as keyboards, drum machines or a MIDI hub. MIDI notes are sent only to the devices you connect.
  • Photos (add only): only if you choose to save a video you made to your photo library. The app cannot read your existing photos.

Sharing

When you share a video or MIDI file, it goes through your device’s share sheet to the app or person you pick. We never receive a copy.

Purchases

If the app offers optional in-app purchases, they are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. Apple handles purchases under its own privacy policy.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@ruebixqueue.com.
