Common questions

How does a word become chords?

The alphabet is lined up with the piano, starting at the lowest key, A. Each letter lands on a note, and each note becomes a chord. Change the key, chord types and rhythm in the settings to hear the same word in new ways.

I can’t hear anything

Turn up the volume, check the mixer’s Master fader, and if you’re using MIDI out, make sure “Play sound here too” is on.

How do I connect a Bluetooth MIDI device?

In MIDI out, tap “Bluetooth devices”, pick your device, then choose it as the Output. Set separate channels for the instrument, bass, lead and drums if your gear needs them.

My drum machine doesn’t play the snare

Some drum machines use their own note numbers. In MIDI, set Drum note map to match your device. Behringer RD-8 and RD-6 are included.

How do I save or share a video?

Tap Share, choose App view or Visualizer, set the number of loops and tap “Record video”. Recording stops on its own. Then tap “Save / share” to send it to Photos or any app.
